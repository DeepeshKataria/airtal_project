### What is Internet Leased Line?

A leased line is a dedicated internet connection with fixed and symmetric bandwidth. It enables small, medium, and large businesses to get internet connecting in a secure, reliable, and highly efficient manner, with maximum download capacity, resilience, and uptime.Airtel’s internet leased line is a private link between companies and the Airtel network. Unlike other connections that multiple users share, an internet leased line connection is exclusive to businesses, ensuring symmetric download and upload speed.It is a high-performance connectivity for modern businesses looking forward to offer seamless connectivity to their large workforce, support remote access to applications, and accelerate cloud adoption. It fuels services like VoIP and scheduled offsite backups, creating room for business growth.

### What is Airtel Internet Leased Line for Business?

Airtel Leased Line Internet is a dedicated and high-performance internet connectivity for your business that you do not share with other businesses. It is a secured, always-on connection for large data exchanges, bandwidth-intensive applications, remote and global connectivity.

### What is the difference between internet leased line and regular broadband?

Regular broadband is an internet connection that multiple organisations share. Sharing affects the connection’s data security, reliability, speed, and bandwidth, leading to fluctuations during peak hours.On the contrary, internet leased line offers exclusive connectivity to your business with dedicated and symmetric bandwidth, ensuring greater security, reliability, and ultra-fast speed.

### What are the different types of internet leased lines?

The different types of internet leased line service are:Fibre leased linesFibre Leased Lines provide a dedicated symmetric data connection by sending light over fibre optic cables. Fiber-optic technology is known for its exceptional data transfer rates, reliability, and security. Fibre leased lines are frequently employed by businesses with demanding needs for high bandwidth and minimal latency, including multimedia firms, research institutions, and financial organizations.DSL leased linesA Digital Subscriber Line (DSL) provides low-bandwidth leased lines. In comparison to fibre leased lines, which are symmetric and non-contended, DSL connections are asymmetric and contended.MPLS leased linesMulti-protocol label switching (MPSL) is a technology for encapsulating data and transferring it from X to Y. You can stick labels on it and then decide on transfers depending on those labels. MPLS is deployed over a leased line circuit, meaning you can obtain an MPLS leased line over cable leased lines, DSL lines, or a combination of both.

### What is the architecture of leased line?

Leased lines are connected directly with the premises or can also be provided through fibre optic cable. In the case of a fiber optic leased line, it works by sending pulses of light. The transmitted data travels at the speed of light.

### How fast is a leased line?

Leased lines often have speeds ranging from 1 Mbps to 10 Gbps. The average speed of a leased line is 2Mbps. However, 10 Mbps and 100 Mbps are becoming more prevalent currently.

### How does Airtel Leased Line Internet work?

A leased line is an allocated circuit between two points of communication. It is always switched on and rented for a monthly charge or according to the service provider’s terms. Leased lines are exclusive, allocated, and dedicated. This means that the associated bandwidth is exclusive for business use.They are different from traditional telecommunications technologies, which use and reuse the circuit by switching. They establish a continuous tunnel between the two communication points, allowing data to flow continuously.Airtel’s leased line connections use the high-capacity, high-speed fibre optics technology. Unlike other connectivity services, it is a closed circuit between the two communication points. The dedicated nature ensures uninterrupted transmission to meet today’s dynamic business needs.It is an always-on service providing a strong and consistent Internet connection and enhancing business operations for bandwidth-hungry applications. Leased lines also work great for businesses dependent on remote or telecommute workforce.

### What are the key factors influencing Leased Line prices?

Some of the key factors that influence leased line pricing are:
- Location: Proximity to existing telecom infrastructure can reduce setup costs. Remote areas may require additional deployment effort, which can impact pricing.
- Bandwidth Needs: Higher bandwidth connections typically cost more. For example, upgrading from 100 Mbps to 1 Gbps will increase the leased line cost.
- Contract Duration: Long-term contracts may come with lower installation fees or bundled offers.
- Redundancy & SLAs: Options like backup links, low-latency routing, and 24/7 support with strong SLAs can also affect overall costs.
- Type of Service: Whether you need just point-to-point connectivity or internet access bundled with the leased line will influence the final quote.
