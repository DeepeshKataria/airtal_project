### What is a telecom carrier service?

A telecom carrier service refers to the infrastructure, connectivity, and network solutions that enable communication at scale, across voice, data, and messaging. Airtel positions its telecom carrier services as tailor-made connectivity solutions designed to help operators navigate the dynamic world of telecommunications. These services empower carriers to expand their reach, drive innovation, reduce costs, optimise resources, navigate complex regulations, and tap into new markets by leveraging Airtel’s infrastructure and expertise.

### What do carrier services do?

Carrier services enable telecom operators to build, expand, and optimise their networks for better performance and wider reach. Airtel’s carrier services help partners virtualise network functions to reduce costs and boost agility, deploy edge computing for low-latency services, expand coverage through strategic partnerships, secure smooth communication and efficient data transmission, generate new revenue streams via global SMS services, and elevate customer experience through low-latency, high-quality connectivity solutions.

### What are carrier services in telecom?

In telecom, carrier services are the wholesale connectivity and infrastructure solutions exchanged between operators to extend network capabilities. ### What is a carrier in telecommunications?

In telecommunications, a carrier is an operator that owns or manages network infrastructure used to transmit voice, data, and messaging traffic, often providing wholesale connectivity to other operators.
